# CapSept Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Welcome to the official, open-source repository for `CapSept`'s privacy policies. The policy is
published at <https://arieltyson.github.io/CapSept-privacy/>.

## Why is this on GitHub?
Trust is built on transparency. By hosting our privacy policy and data mapping documentation in a public Git repository, we ensure that:
1. **Historical Tracking:** You can view the commit history to see exactly when and how our privacy practices change.
2. **Technical Honesty:** Our `DATA_MAPPING.md` file technically aligns with our legal `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`.
3. **Public Accountability:** If our app behavior deviates from this policy, the community can hold us accountable.

## What CapSept does with your data
- Your answers, writing, study plan and scores stay on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud account. The developer cannot read them.
- Voice recordings are transcribed on your iPhone and deleted after scoring unless you choose to keep them; kept recordings are deleted after 30 days.
- Writing and speaking feedback is produced on your iPhone by Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent to a server.
- Family Mode shares only your name, streak, weekly minutes and predicted levels, and only with the people you invite.
- There are no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party SDKs.

## Reporting a Privacy Concern
If you discover that `CapSept` is handling data in a way that contradicts this policy, or if you have found a security vulnerability:
1. Open a new **Issue** in this repository.
2. Tag it with the `privacy-violation` or `security` label.
3. Provide steps to reproduce the data leak or tracker usage.
